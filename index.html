About Havya IFM

A new standard for how buildings are cared for.

Havya IFM is built on more than 15 years of hands-on experience in facility management across a wide range of industries. Our approach is rooted in green building principles and energy-saving practices — helping clients run efficient, sustainable properties while creating safe, healthy environments for everyone who uses them.

We manage, operate, supervise and maintain commercial buildings, offices, residential communities, hospitals, hotels, guest houses and farmhouses. Technical teams, soft-service crews and hospitality staff work to one plan, report into one dashboard and answer to one contract.
